What's inside

Everything the front desk and the vets need, without the paper.

Dashboard

Today's appointments, vaccines coming due, low stock and unpaid invoices on one screen when you open the app.

Clients and pets

Full history for every animal: visits, vaccinations, medications, illnesses and notes, linked to the owner.

Appointments

A calendar for the whole team, with automatic reminder emails the day before and follow-ups after the visit.

Invoices and payments

Turn a visit into an invoice in a few taps, take partial payments, print a PDF and see who still owes what.

Inventory and suppliers

Medicines and products with stock levels, purchase orders, supplier balances and a warning before you run out.

Statistics and expenses

Revenue, profit after expenses, best-selling services and monthly trends, with CSV export for your accountant.
